BLAZING REVIVAL — NINJA ROAD v1.4 (Discord Screenshots)

1. Extract whole ZIP to a folder in your personal PC account.
2. Double-click Run-Windows.bat (Python 3.10+ required).
3. Open Naruto Blazing Revival and select the upright, game-only area.
4. For automatic Discord updates, paste a private Discord webhook URL under
   'Discord screenshots', check 'Send after every run', and Save.
5. Use 'Send test screenshot' to test your webhook.
6. For a remote /screenshot command, create and invite your own Discord BOT
   via Discord Developer Portal. Add Bot Token, Server ID, Your User ID and
   optional Channel ID. Press 'Connect Discord bot'.
7. Open Discord from your phone or PC and enter /screenshot in that server.
   Only the approved User ID can request images. Replies are private.
8. Uncheck 'Dry Run' and Start to automate Ninja Road.

Discord setup details and security notes: see DISCORD-SETUP.md.
Webhook alone cannot receive commands; /screenshot REQUIRES a bot.
Keep the app running, emulator visible, and your PC awake for remote requests.
Do NOT share discord_config.json (contains Discord secrets in plain text).
